        The house edge is real, even here

        The games use realistic odds, so they behave like the real thing: the longer you play, the more your balance tends to drift down. Roulette keeps 2.7% of every bet on average, Pixel Reels about 6% before the jackpot, Pixel Crossing about 3%, Plinko about 3.5%, cases about 8%, and coin flip 2%. In a real casino that drift is paid in real money. Watching it happen with pretend credits is one of the most useful things a simulation can show.

        If gambling is affecting you

        In the UK, the National Gambling Helpline is 0808 8020 133, run by GamCare.
